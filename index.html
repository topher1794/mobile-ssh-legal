Privacy Policy for Mobile SSH

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

Mobile SSH (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Mobile SSH mobile application (the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

Mobile SSH does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, analytics, or usage telemetry on external developer-controlled servers.

  • Connection Profiles & Credentials: Hostnames, IP addresses, ports, usernames, passwords, and SSH private keys that you configure in the App are stored locally on your device solely to enable you to connect to your own servers.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the App.

2. How Information Is Used

All connection settings and authentication credentials stored in the App are used strictly on your device to establish direct Secure Shell (SSH) network connections between your device and the remote servers you specify.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All saved host profiles, passwords, and SSH keys remain locally within your device’s application storage.
  • We do not have access to your saved hosts, login credentials, private keys, or terminal session contents.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its local app data will permanently delete all locally stored connection profiles and keys from your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

  • No Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.
  • No Third-Party Ads or Analytics: The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking tools.
  • Direct Server Connections: When you initiate an SSH connection, network traffic is transmitted directly between your device and the destination server you configured. Your interaction with those remote servers is governed by the policies and security practices of the respective server administrators.

5. Children’s Privacy

Mobile SSH is a technical utility intended for general adult and professional use and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
